Figures from papers

Hau et al. 2025

H.-M. Hau et al., “Disordered Rocksalts as High-Energy and Earth-Abundant Li-Ion Cathodes,” Advanced Materials 37 (2025), 2502766. doi.org/10.1002/adma.202502766

© 2025 The Authors. CC BY 4.0.

Used: Figure 1 and a quoted passage from Section 1, on the homepage and in Rocksalt Li pathways. Changed: nothing in the figure’s pixels. Fig.3D adds its own outline, dimming, zoom and arrows, lifts pieces of the figure into 3D, and adapts its models from it.

He, Zhu & Mo 2017

Xingfeng He, Yizhou Zhu & Yifei Mo, “Origin of fast ion diffusion in super-ionic conductors,” Nature Communications 8, 15893 (2017). doi.org/10.1038/ncomms15893

© The Authors 2017. CC BY 4.0.

Used: Figures 1 to 4, and energy values read from Figure 3, in Lithium that moves together. Changed: the figures are rasterised from the author PDF without repainting, and some panels are shown as crops. The outlines and the moving marker are Fig.3D’s.

Kang et al. 2024

Y. Kang et al., “Effect of crystal facets in plasmonic catalysis,” Nature Communications 15, 3923 (2024). doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-47994-y

© The Authors 2024. CC BY 4.0.

Used: Figure 1 in Inside a nanoparticle; panels a to f are shown beside the model and the complete figure is linked. Changed: panels are cropped losslessly. The outlines over them are Fig.3D’s.
